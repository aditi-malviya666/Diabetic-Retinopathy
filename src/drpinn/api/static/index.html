The Problem & Our Idea in One Page

The problem

Diabetic retinopathy silently damages retinal vessels and is a leading cause of blindness. Grading by hand is slow, costly and varies between doctors, and a grade alone does not say where to look first.

Our idea in plain words

A basic AI only says the stage: "Stage 2". Ours does that too, then goes further: it traces the blood vessels, estimates the strain on each part using the patient's normal arm blood pressure, and highlights the regions most likely to leak or bleed next, with a confidence score. Like a doctor who gives the diagnosis and also marks where to look first.

Basic AI: "Stage 2" Ours: Stage 2 + where + how sure

Sweet tooth can be a silent threat to your eyes.

Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) develops silently without pain or early vision symptoms. Chronic high blood glucose weakens the microvascular walls of retinal capillaries, leading to leakages, hemorrhages, and irreversible sight loss if left undetected.

Early Screening Saves Sight Annual Exam Recommended
The 4 Clinical Stages of Diabetic Retinopathy
Stage 1
Mild Non-Proliferative
Tiny balloon-like microaneurysms emerge in retinal capillary walls. Fluid can leak into surrounding tissue.
Action: Annual screening.
Stage 2
Moderate NPDR
Vessel occlusions multiply, starving retinal patches of normal oxygen and nutrients. Hard lipid exudates form.
Action: Glycemic & BP control.
Stage 3
Severe NPDR
Widespread capillary drop-out. Venous beading and extensive intraretinal hemorrhages signal high progression risk.
Action: 3-4 month monitoring.
Stage 4
Proliferative DR (PDR)
Severe hypoxia triggers fragile new vessels (neovascularization) that bleed into the vitreous humor causing sudden blindness.
Action: URGENT laser / anti-VEGF.
